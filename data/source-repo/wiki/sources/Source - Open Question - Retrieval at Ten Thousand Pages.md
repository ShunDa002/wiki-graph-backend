# Source - Open Question - Retrieval at Ten Thousand Pages

## What this source is

Synthetic test note that deliberately poses a question the vault cannot yet answer.

## Claims taken from it

None — the source is a question plus a list of evidence gaps, not a claim. Recorded as-is rather
than forced into a concept:

- Question: what retrieval architecture gives acceptable precision, recall, latency, and cost at
  10,000 pages with multiple agents maintaining the vault?
- Evidence the vault does not have: a large-vault dataset, a stable benchmark at that scale, a
  comparison of full-text/metadata/graph/semantic retrieval at that scale, concurrency and
  index-refresh testing, cost and latency measurements.

## What it changed in the Wiki

- No concept created — there is no claim here to compile, only a gap. See the note under "Not
  used" below.
- Added to `wiki/index.md` under Open questions.

## Not used

No concept page was created for this source, on purpose. A concept page states something that
can be explained; this source explicitly states what is *not yet known*. Forcing it into a
concept template would manufacture a claim the vault has no basis for. It belongs in the index as
an open question instead — a page will be worth creating once there is an answer, or at least a
benchmark result, to record.
